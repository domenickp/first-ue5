# Story

## Premise
A demonic evil is spreading across a land from a castle at its heart. A lone hunter sets out
to destroy it.

## Themes
- Becoming the thing you hunt
- The cost of power, and how gaining it corrupts
- Objectivity vs. intimacy: the camera moves from watching the hero to *being* them

## How the story is told
Mostly through the camera, the music and events, rather than dialogue. There is one
scripted moment where the player loses control (the weapon pickup) because it is
critical to the narrative.
