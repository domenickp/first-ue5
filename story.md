# Story

## Premise
A demonic evil is spreading across a land from a castle at its heart. A lone hunter sets out
to destroy it.

## Themes
- Becoming the thing you hunt
- The cost of power, and how gaining it corrupts
- Objectivity vs. intimacy: the camera moves from watching the hero to *being* them

## How the story is told
Mostly through the camera, the music and events, rather than dialogue. There is one
scripted moment where the player loses control (the weapon pickup) because it is
critical to the narrative.

## Choices & branching
None. The story is linear.

## Ending(s)
This level is a **pivotal chapter**, not the whole story. It ends with the player, now a demon,
smashing a personal artifact and pledging fealty to a powerful demon, who will assign them tasks.
That's the hook into the levels that come after.

## Wider story (future levels)
More levels are planned **before and after** this one. This level is the turning point where the
hunter becomes a demon.

## Key scenes

### Scene: The boss's roar
The boss fully emerges and roars. The player is thrown through the air, their view rotates
to the side, they slam into a fence, take damage, and get back up to begin the fight.

### Scene: The transformation
It should be dramatic. This is a key plot moment. First draft, to be developed further:
1. The player is lifted into the air and hangs there for a moment.
2. Purple, searing pentagrams burn onto their forearms. In first person, the player sees this
   happening on their own arms.
3. They shout in pain.
4. They drop to the ground, land on their feet, and play resumes.
