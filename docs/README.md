## Tips
- **Priority tags:** mark ideas as `[MUST]`, `[WANT]` or `[DREAM]`.
- **Unknowns:** write `TBD` or `?` and keep going. Gaps are fine.
- **Concrete over abstract:** "the player hears footsteps above them in the dark" beats "the game is scary".
- **References are shorthand:** name the games, films, books and art that capture the *feel* you want.
- The `<!-- ... -->` comments are hints. They don't show up when the file is viewed on GitHub, and you can delete them once you've filled in a section.
